## Personnel orientation workflow
1. HR opens an approved requisition and job description; obtain authorization to recruit.
2. Verify identity and employment authorization using the applicable official processes, without placing sensitive identifiers in general templates.
3. Confirm state licensure, education, certification and privilege checks as applicable from primary sources.
4. Obtain an occupational health clearance status only; restrict underlying health information.
5. Assign hospital-wide orientation, including patient rights, emergency response, infection prevention, safety, privacy, QAPI and records.
6. Assign department-specific topics based on job classification and hospital services.
7. Assign supervision and observed competence checks when required.
8. Capture true assessor identity, assessment outcome, evidence reference, policy version, deficiencies and corrective plan.
9. Obtain supervisor review and valid employee attestations; training completion is not equivalent to independent privileges.
10. Monitor updates, expiration and recurrent education according to specific requirements and hospital policy.

## HR4000 digital acceptance and safety gates
- Forms are initially empty and must never pre-populate signatures, verification outcomes, employee achievements, or medical details.
- Real production requires secure personnel storage, tenant isolation, field-level restrictions, documented retention, access logs, electronic signatures where valid and controlled records exports.
- Credentialing and role scope must be independently verified by qualified staff; agent-generated text is not evidence.
- Each 900-form template must be tested for appropriate role-specific fields, audit events, appropriate permissions, mobile accessibility, review workflow, exception handling and PDF/HTML output.
- Obtain independent review for federal/state requirements, hospital scope, Joint Commission standards (separate licensed sources), infection prevention, employment law and hospital governance.

**Implementation status:** 900-form template generator, private catalog endpoints and orientation viewer exist in a feature branch. There is not yet evidence of a deployed HR records system or independently validated regulatory crosswalk.
