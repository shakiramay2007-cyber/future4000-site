01

What a cloud platform is

A platform that runs your software on rented, managed computers so your team ships code instead of maintaining servers.

02

Why healthcare needs its own platform

Patient data needs access control, audit trails and a signed BAA from day one, not as a premium upgrade.

03

Who F4 Cloud serves

Health systems, health-tech builders, universities and government agencies that handle regulated data.

04

The service catalog

Web services, static sites, private services, background workers, cron jobs, workflows, Postgres, key-value stores and sandboxes.

05

How a deploy works

Push to GitHub, we build it, deploy it to new instances, health-check it and switch traffic with zero downtime.

06

HIPAA on every plan

Every paid plan runs on HIPAA-eligible AWS services with encryption, audit logs and a BAA, with no compliance surcharge.

07

Patient data kept out of logs

Logs, build output and error reports are scrubbed for identifiers automatically.

08

Private networking by default

Services talk over a private network, and private links to your AWS accounts are included on every plan.

09

Alerts and webhooks

Slack, Microsoft Teams, email and webhooks for deploys, failures and recoveries on every plan.

10

Observability

Metrics, logs, traces, health checks and uptime in one dashboard, exportable with OpenTelemetry.

11

Preview copies and rollback

A full test copy for every pull request using synthetic data, and one-click rollback to any earlier build.

12

Autoscaling and zero-downtime deploys

Instances scale with load, and new versions take traffic only after passing health checks.

13

Healthcare connectors

FHIR, Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, EVV and clearinghouse connectors, with every transfer logged.

14

AI with governance

Claude on AWS Bedrock with PHI masking, an AI inventory, a decision log and a pause switch.

15

The security program

Encryption, least-privilege access, SSO with MFA, secrets management, DDoS protection, pentests and a SOC 2 roadmap.

16

Pricing models

Free for learning, Team, Compliance and Enterprise tiers. Final prices are being set during early access.

17

Common questions

HIPAA-eligible does not mean automatically compliant: we secure the platform, you configure and use it correctly.
