Frequently asked questions

Does FUTURE4000 replace our EHR?

No. It works alongside Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth and others, and fixes the compliance, policy and training gaps around them.

Can AI-written policies go straight into use?

No. Every AI draft is a draft until a qualified clinician approves it, and authors cannot approve their own work. Every step is audit-logged.

Do you cover state regulations?

Federal and accreditation requirements are mapped automatically. State rules vary widely, so the Regulation Mapper drafts them for your compliance officer to confirm.

Can we bring the policies we already have?

Yes. Upload them and Policy Gap Review compares each one against your mapped requirements and lists the fixes.

How do surveyors see our evidence?

Export the audit log, policy versions with approvals, and training completion reports in one click.
